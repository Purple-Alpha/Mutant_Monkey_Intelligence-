# Pricing Model

## Current Pricing
$199-$399/month depending on employee count and scope.

## Rationale
- Simple
- Predictable
- SMB-friendly
- Easy to explain in sales calls

## Suggested Tiers

### Essentials
For small teams that need a simple monthly phishing simulation and basic reporting.

### Standard
For growing SMBs that need simulations, micro-training, monthly reports, and evidence packaging.

### Plus
For higher-risk teams that need leadership summaries, repeat-clicker tracking, and optional quarterly briefings.
