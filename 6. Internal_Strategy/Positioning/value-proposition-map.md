# Value Proposition Map

## Customer Jobs
- Protect staff
- Meet insurance requirements
- Reduce phishing risk
- Show leadership that action is being taken

## Pains
- Employees click phishing emails
- No consistent training
- No leadership-ready reporting
- Enterprise tools are too expensive or complex

## Gains
- Monthly simulations
- Micro-training
- Evidence packages
- Simple monthly service
- Plain-English security guidance

## Proof Points
- Safe, non-credential-harvesting simulations
- Canadian SMB focus
- Insurance-friendly documentation
- No software rollout required
