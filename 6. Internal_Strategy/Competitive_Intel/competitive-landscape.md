# Competitive Landscape

## Categories
- MSP add-ons
- Awareness training vendors
- Enterprise phishing tools
- Cyber insurance portals

## NorthStar Advantage
- Canadian data focus
- AI-powered workflow
- Insurance-friendly reporting
- No software rollout
- Plain-English delivery for SMBs

## Risks
- Larger vendors may appear more established.
- MSPs may bundle awareness training with existing IT services.
- SMBs may delay buying until insurance or incident pressure increases.

## Response
Position NorthStar as the simple, focused, human-layer security service for SMB leaders who want action and evidence without enterprise complexity.
