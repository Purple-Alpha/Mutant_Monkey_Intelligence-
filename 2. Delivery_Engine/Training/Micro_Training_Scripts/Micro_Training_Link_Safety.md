# Micro-Training Module
## Topic: Link Safety — Hover Before You Click
NorthStar Security — Human-Layer Training
Duration: 2–4 minutes

---

## 1. Why This Matters
Most phishing attacks succeed because of malicious links.
Attackers disguise harmful URLs to look legitimate.

This module teaches you how to identify unsafe links instantly.

---

## 2. How Attackers Trick You
- They use look-alike domains
  Example: `micr0soft-secure.com`
- They hide links behind buttons
- They use shortened URLs
- They mimic internal tools or login pages

---

## 3. The Hover Test (Your #1 Defense)
Before clicking any link:

### Hover your mouse over the link
A small preview will appear in the bottom-left of your screen.

### Ask yourself:
- Does the domain look correct?
- Is it spelled properly?
- Does it match the sender?
- Is it a site you normally use?

If anything feels off — don’t click.

---

## 4. Safe vs Unsafe Examples
### Safe:
`https://microsoft.com/security`

### Unsafe:
`https://microsoft-verify-login.com/auth`

### Safe:
`https://yourcompany.com/hr`

### Unsafe:
`https://yourcompany-records-update.net`

---

## 5. What To Do Next Time
- Hover before clicking
- Verify the domain
- Report suspicious links
- When unsure, ask your internal contact

---

## 6. Final Reminder
Attackers rely on quick clicks.
Slow down, hover, and stay safe.
