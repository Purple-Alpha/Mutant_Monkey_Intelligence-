# Micro-Training Module
## Topic: Click Awareness — Recognizing Suspicious Emails
NorthStar Security — Human-Layer Training
Duration: 2–3 minutes

---

## 1. What Happened
You clicked on a simulated phishing email during this month’s security test.
This training will help you quickly identify similar threats in the future.

---

## 2. Key Red Flags You Missed
- The sender address didn’t match the real department
- The link pointed to an unfamiliar domain
- The message created a sense of urgency
- The email used generic greetings instead of your name

---

## 3. How to Spot Phishing in Under 5 Seconds
### A. Check the Sender
Does the email come from the correct domain?
Example: `@company.com` vs `@company-support.com`

### B. Hover Over Links
Before clicking, hover your mouse to preview the URL.
If it looks strange, don’t click.

### C. Slow Down
Phishing relies on speed.
Take 3 seconds to scan before acting.

---

## 4. What To Do Next Time
- Don’t click links immediately
- Verify the sender
- Report suspicious emails to your internal contact
- When in doubt, ask — it’s always the right move

---

## 5. Final Reminder
This was a safe simulation.
Real attackers use the same tactics — but with harmful intent.
Your awareness is the strongest defense.
