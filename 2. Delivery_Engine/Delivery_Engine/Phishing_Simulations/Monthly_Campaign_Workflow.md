# Monthly Campaign Workflow

## 1. Monthly Planning

- Confirm client scope and approved simulation boundaries
- Review employee list and any staffing updates
- Confirm excluded users, dates, or departments
- Select the campaign theme for the month
- Choose whether the simulation will be broad or role-targeted

## 2. Scenario Selection

- Choose a phishing scenario that matches the client’s industry or current risk profile
- Use safe, non-credential-harvesting simulations only
- Avoid prohibited content such as malware, emotional distress, or sensitive HR topics
- Match the simulation difficulty to the client’s maturity level

## 3. Campaign Setup

- Prepare recipient list
- Assign simulation template
- Confirm delivery timing
- Verify reporting recipients
- Confirm leadership contacts for evidence delivery

## 4. Simulation Launch

- Launch the phishing simulation on the scheduled date
- Deliver to all staff or selected groups depending on scope
- Monitor delivery status and participation activity
- Track clicks and engagement safely

## 5. Follow-Up Micro-Training

- Send 2 to 4 minute follow-up training to employees who clicked
- Reinforce recognition of phishing, impersonation, invoice fraud, and AI-generated lures
- Keep training short, practical, and behaviour-focused

## 6. Reporting

- Compile click rates
- Identify repeat-clickers
- Review department-level trends
- Summarize findings in plain English
- Add practical recommendations for leadership

## 7. Leadership Evidence Package

- Include proof of simulation
- Include proof of follow-up training
- Include proof of corrective action
- Prepare insurance-friendly documentation where needed

## 8. Monthly Review

- Review results with the client
- Discuss trends, weak spots, and improvement areas
- Recommend any add-ons if needed
- Prepare the next month’s campaign direction
