# Simulation Execution Checklist

## Before Launch

- Confirm signed authorization is on file
- Confirm scope and approved simulation type
- Verify employee list is current
- Confirm excluded users or protected groups
- Confirm reporting recipients
- Confirm delivery date and time
- Review template for accuracy and appropriateness
- Ensure simulation follows policy boundaries

## Launch Day

- Activate the simulation
- Verify delivery has started correctly
- Confirm no prohibited content is included
- Monitor early activity for delivery issues
- Log any technical or operational problems

## During Campaign

- Monitor click activity
- Track targeted group performance if applicable
- Watch for unexpected issues or client concerns
- Maintain internal notes for reporting

## After Campaign

- Export campaign results
- Identify clickers and repeat-clickers
- Trigger follow-up micro-training for clickers
- Compile monthly report findings
- Prepare leadership evidence package
- Save documentation to the client record

## Quality Control

- Confirm no credentials were harvested
- Confirm no malware or payloads were used
- Confirm no emotional distress or prohibited scenarios were used
- Confirm reporting is accurate and readable
- Confirm training follow-up was completed

## Client Follow-Up

- Send monthly report
- Send leadership evidence package
- Schedule review if needed
- Note recommendations for next month’s campaign
