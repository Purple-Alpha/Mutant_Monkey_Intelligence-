# Tool Stack Notes

## Core Delivery Stack

- Email delivery platform for phishing simulations
- Reporting workflow for click tracking and leadership summaries
- Secure document handling for evidence packages and checklists
- Internal template library for campaigns, reports, and training content

## Operational Requirements

- Support safe, non-credential-harvesting simulations
- Allow segmentation by department, role, or targeted user group
- Support monthly campaign scheduling
- Allow repeatable reporting and documentation workflows
- Keep delivery simple for small and mid-sized business clients

## Recommended Functional Categories

### Simulation Delivery

Tools in this category should support:
- Email campaign delivery
- Template management
- Recipient segmentation
- Tracking of opens, clicks, and participation

### Training Delivery

Tools in this category should support:
- Short follow-up training for clickers
- Simple access without heavy onboarding
- Easy reuse of training modules each month

### Reporting and Documentation

Tools in this category should support:
- Monthly summary generation
- Leadership-ready exports
- Insurance-friendly evidence records
- Internal workflow consistency

### Operations and Admin

Tools in this category should support:
- Client onboarding workflows
- Checklist tracking
- Scope confirmation
- Renewal and monthly review processes

## Tool Selection Notes

When choosing tools, prioritize:
- Simplicity
- Low operating cost
- Clear reporting
- Reliable email delivery
- Easy reuse across multiple SMB clients
- Canadian-friendly data handling where possible

## Internal Standard

The tool stack should stay lean and practical.

Avoid unnecessary complexity, oversized enterprise platforms, or anything that makes monthly delivery harder than it needs to be.
