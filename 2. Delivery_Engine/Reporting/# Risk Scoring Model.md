# Risk Scoring Model  
NorthStar Security — Human‑Layer Risk Framework  
Version 1.0

## Purpose
This model provides a simple, transparent scoring system to measure human‑layer phishing risk across the organization.

## Scoring Categories
### 1. Click Rate (40%)
- 0–10% = Low Risk (0 points)
- 11–20% = Moderate Risk (10 points)
- 21–30% = High Risk (20 points)
- 31%+ = Critical Risk (30 points)

### 2. Repeat Clickers (30%)
- 0 repeat clickers = 0 points
- 1–2 repeat clickers = 10 points
- 3–4 repeat clickers = 20 points
- 5+ repeat clickers = 30 points

### 3. Department Risk (20%)
- No department above 20% click rate = 0 points
- One department above 20% = 10 points
- Two or more departments above 20% = 20 points

### 4. Training Completion (10%)
- 90–100% = 0 points
- 70–89% = 5 points
- Below 70% = 10 points

## Total Score Interpretation
- **0–20 = Low Risk**
- **21–40 = Moderate Risk**
- **41–60 = High Risk**
- **61–80 = Critical Risk**

## Notes
- Simple enough for SMB leadership  
- Insurance‑friendly  
- No personal shaming
