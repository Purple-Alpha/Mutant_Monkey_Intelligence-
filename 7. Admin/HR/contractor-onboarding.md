# Contractor Onboarding Checklist

## Before Access
- NDA signed
- Role confirmed
- Scope of work documented
- Data handling expectations reviewed

## Access
- Email or workspace access granted
- Tool permissions assigned
- Client data access limited to need-to-know

## Training
- NorthStar service overview completed
- Privacy expectations reviewed
- Reporting workflow reviewed

## Offboarding
- Access removed
- Files returned or transferred
- Client data deleted from contractor devices
