# Brand Guidelines

## Voice
- Plain-English
- Helpful
- Calm
- Non-technical
- Trust-focused

## Messaging
NorthStar should sound practical, protective, and easy to understand. Avoid fear-heavy language and jargon.

## Visual Direction
- Clean
- Minimal
- Professional
- Evidence-oriented
- Suitable for Canadian SMB leadership

## Brand Rule
The brand should make security feel manageable, not overwhelming.
