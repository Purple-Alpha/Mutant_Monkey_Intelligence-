# Simulation Policy Boundaries  
NorthStar Security

## Allowed Simulation Types
- Email phishing  
- Fake invoice/payment requests  
- Executive impersonation  
- AI‑generated phishing templates  
- Industry‑specific lures  

## Prohibited Simulation Types
- Credential harvesting  
- Malware or payloads  
- Attachments  
- Exploits  
- Ransomware simulations  
- Sensitive HR topics (discipline, layoffs, medical issues)  
- Emotional distress scenarios  

## Ethical Standards
- No shaming  
- No public reporting of individuals  
- No disciplinary‑style simulations
