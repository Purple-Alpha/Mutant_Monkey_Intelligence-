# Onboarding Checklist  
NorthStar Security

## Before Kickoff
- Signed authorization  
- Employee list received  
- Exclusion dates confirmed  
- High‑risk roles identified  

## Kickoff Call
- Review scope  
- Confirm timing  
- Confirm reporting recipients  

## First Simulation
- Template selection  
- Schedule confirmation  
- Delivery  

## After Simulation
- Micro‑training sent  
- Monthly report drafted  
- Leadership evidence prepared
