## AI Phishing Essentials Offer Sheet

### Overview

AI Phishing Essentials is a monthly managed security service designed for Canadian small and mid-sized businesses with 5 to 50 employees.

It is built to provide simple, affordable, and effective protection against phishing, impersonation, invoice fraud, SMS scams, and AI-generated social engineering.

### What’s Included (Monthly)

#### A. Phishing Simulation

- 1 safe, non-credential-harvesting simulation per month
- Industry-specific templates
- AI-generated lures
- Delivered to all staff or targeted groups

#### B. Follow-Up Micro-Training

- 2 to 4 minute training
- Sent only to employees who clicked
- Reinforces safe behaviour

#### C. Monthly Human-Risk Report

- Click rates
- Repeat-clickers
- Department-level risk
- Recommendations
- Insurance-friendly evidence

#### D. Incident-Reporting Checklist

- One-page quick-response guide
- Steps for finance, admin, and leadership

#### E. Leadership Evidence Package

- Proof of simulation
- Proof of training
- Proof of corrective action
