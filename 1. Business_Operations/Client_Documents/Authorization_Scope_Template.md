## **3. Authorized Activities**

NorthStar Security is authorized to perform the following activities on behalf of the Client:

### **A. Monthly Phishing Simulation**

- One (1) phishing simulation per month

- Delivered to all employees or a defined subset

- Uses **safe, non‑credential‑harvesting** templates

- No malware, payloads, or harmful content

### **B. Follow‑Up Micro‑Training**

- Sent only to employees who click

- 2–4 minute training module

- Reinforces safe behaviour

### **C. Monthly Human‑Risk Report**

- Click rates

- Repeat‑clicker identification

- Department‑level risk

- Recommendations for leadership

- Insurance‑friendly evidence

### **D. Leadership Evidence Package**

- Proof of simulation

- Proof of training

- Proof of corrective action

## **4. Scope of Simulations**

NorthStar simulations may include:

- Email‑based phishing

- Fake invoice or payment‑request scenarios

- Impersonation attempts (CEO, CFO, HR, IT)

- AI‑generated phishing templates

- SMS‑style or mobile‑style templates (email‑only delivery)

- Industry‑specific lures (real estate, finance, healthcare, trades, legal, etc.)

Simulations will **not** include:

- Credential harvesting

- Malware links

- Attachments

- Exploits

- Ransomware simulations

- Any action that could cause operational disruption

## **5. Employee List & Targeting**

Client agrees to provide:

- A list of employee email addresses

- Department or role information (optional)

- Identification of high‑risk roles (optional)

NorthStar agrees to:

- Use employee data **only** for simulation and reporting

- Never share, sell, or store data outside Canada

- Delete data upon request

## **6. Timing & Frequency**

- One simulation per month

- Delivered during business hours

- No simulations on statutory holidays

- No simulations during known operationally sensitive periods (payroll, audits, major events)

Client may request:

- A specific week or day

- Exclusion windows

- Targeted campaigns for executives or finance

## **7. Safety & Ethical Boundaries**

NorthStar adheres to strict ethical guidelines:

- No shaming or blaming employees

- No public reporting of individual results

- No simulations involving sensitive topics (HR complaints, layoffs, medical issues, harassment, etc.)

- No simulations that mimic internal disciplinary actions

- No simulations that could cause emotional distress

## **8. Reporting & Evidence**

NorthStar will provide:

### **Monthly Report**

- Click rates

- Repeat‑clickers

- Department risk

- Recommendations

### **Leadership Evidence Package**

- Simulation summary

- Training completion

- Insurance‑friendly documentation

### **Optional Add‑Ons**

- **Quarterly AI Threat Briefing**

- **Incident‑Response Tabletop**

- **AI Usage & Security Policy**

## **9. Client Responsibilities**

Client agrees to:

- Provide accurate employee lists

- Notify NorthStar of major staffing changes

- Inform NorthStar of blackout periods

- Communicate internally that simulations may occur

- Ensure leadership is aware of the program

## **10. Data Handling & Privacy**

NorthStar will:

- Store all data in Canada

- Use data only for simulations and reporting

- Delete data upon termination

- Never access client systems or inboxes

- Never request credentials

## **11. Term & Cancellation**

- Month‑to‑month service

- Cancel anytime with 30 days’ notice

- No long‑term contracts
