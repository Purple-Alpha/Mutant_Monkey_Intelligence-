# **Unified Folder Structure — NorthStar + SwarmCommand Venture**

**NorthStar Security — AI‑Powered Human‑Layer Protection for Canadian SMBs**
**Powered by SwarmCommand Internal Automation**

> **Single source of truth** for planning, execution, and future development of the unified NorthStar + SwarmCommand venture.

---

## **Operating Principle**

This venture runs on one principle, three layers:

- **NorthStar** is the **commercial business** — what we sell, deliver, and get paid for today.
- **SwarmCommand** is the **internal engine** — the automation that lets a small team operate like a large one.
- **The 60‑agent platform** is the **long‑term product** — what the engine becomes once productized.

Every artifact in this Space must serve at least one of those three layers.

---

## **The "One‑Thing" Filter**

Only add new items to this Space if they **directly support** one of the following:

1. **One offer**
2. **One buyer**
3. **One outreach motion**
4. **One delivery workflow**
5. **First paying clients**

If a proposed artifact does not move one of those forward, it does not belong in this Space yet.

## **What Each Section Is For**

### **1. Business_Operations**

The commercial surface of NorthStar. Lives here:

- Offers and pricing
- Client documents (e.g. `Authorization_Scope_Template.md`)
- Outreach assets (cold email sequences, scripts)
- Website copy
- Reports templates

### **2. Delivery_Engine**

How NorthStar actually delivers the service month over month:

- Phishing simulations and campaign examples
- Micro‑training scripts
- Reporting workflows (monthly human‑risk report, leadership evidence package)

### **3. SwarmCommand_Engine**

The internal automation that powers delivery and operations:

- **Workflow_Agents** — automate delivery steps
- **Scoring_Agents** — analyze click/risk data
- **Drafting_Agents** — generate outreach, training, reports
- **Experiments** — detection sandbox, R&D
- **Future_Platform_Agents** — staging area for the 60‑agent platform

### **4. Product_Roadmap**

Where we are going:

- 90‑day plan (first paying clients)
- 6–12 month plan (scale + product loops)
- 60‑agent platform vision
- Architecture diagrams

### **5. Clients**

One folder per client. Standard subfolders:

- `Discovery` — kickoff notes, employee list intake
- `Campaigns` — monthly simulations
- `Evidence` — leadership / insurance‑friendly package
- `Reports` — monthly human‑risk reports
- `Renewal` — renewal conversation prep

### **6. Internal_Strategy**

Why we win and against whom:

- Vision and positioning
- Competitive analysis
- Market research (Canadian SMB segment)

### **7. Admin**

Keep the business legal, paid, and on‑brand:

- Legal
- Finance
- Branding (incl. Logo)

## **Conventions**

- **Folder names** are numbered (`1.`, `2.`, …) to enforce a stable top‑down reading order.
- **Filenames** use `Snake_Case_With_Capitals.md` (e.g. `Authorization_Scope_Template.md`).
- **Companion docs**: a `*.rd.md` next to a `*.md` is the short *rich description* (intro / readme) for the same page.
- **Drafts** are versioned in the title (e.g. `Draft v1.0`) until promoted.
- **Client data** lives only inside `5. Clients/<ClientName_##>/` — never at the root.
