# SwarmCommand Workflows — Overview

SwarmCommand Engine — Workflow Layer

## Purpose

Workflows define the multi‑agent processes that power the future autonomous platform.  
Each workflow describes:

- The goal

- The agents involved

- The sequence of steps

- Inputs and outputs

Workflows are **not code** — they are operational blueprints.

## Workflow Categories

- Simulation Workflows

- Training Workflows

- Reporting Workflows

- Evidence Workflows

- Optimization Workflows

## Status

Conceptual — for future platform development.
