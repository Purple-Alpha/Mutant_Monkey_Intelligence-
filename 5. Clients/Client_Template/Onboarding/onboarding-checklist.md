# Client Onboarding Checklist

## Required
- Client legal name
- Primary contact
- Billing contact
- Employee contact list
- Authorized scope signed
- Simulation schedule confirmed
- Blackout dates confirmed

## Technical Setup
- Sending domain or platform confirmed
- Test delivery completed
- Allowlisting instructions sent if needed

## Preferences
- Training preferences
- Reporting cadence
- Leadership summary recipients
- Sensitive topics to avoid

## Ready to Launch
- Campaign date selected
- Template selected
- Evidence folder created
- Internal notes updated
