# NorthStar Specialization Roadmap
### Fraud Detection + Ransomware Defense

## Purpose
This is the strategic specialization roadmap for the NorthStar SwarmCommand venture. It commits NorthStar to a thematic position — **human-layer fraud and ransomware defense** — rather than the broader "AI email analysis" or "generic phishing defense" framing. It sequences the work in four phases that build on each other: Foundation, Capability Expansion, Market Positioning, Monetization & Scale.

This document is the *strategic intent* layer of the roadmap. It does not list weekly build tasks — those live in `12_Week_Timeline.md` and in `PROJECT_HANDSHAKE.md`. This doc tells the future operator *which mountain we are climbing*; the others tell them *which step is next on the trail*.
