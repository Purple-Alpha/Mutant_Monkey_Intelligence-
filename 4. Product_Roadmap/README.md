## **Horizons**

### **Horizon 1 — Next 90 Days: "First Paying Clients"**

Lock the operating principle in revenue. Success = signed clients running the Delivery Workflow.

- [ ] One buyer profile published.
- [ ] One outreach motion live (cold email sequence v1).
- [ ] One delivery workflow documented end‑to‑end.
- [ ] First paying client onboarded.
- [ ] Second and third clients onboarded.
- [ ] Monthly Human‑Risk Report shipped to all active clients.

### **Horizon 2 — Months 4–12: "Scale + Product Loops"**

Turn the delivery into a repeatable, mostly‑automated motion.

- [ ] SwarmCommand `Workflow_Agent: Monthly Delivery` in production.
- [ ] `Scoring_Agent: Click Risk` producing reports unattended.
- [ ] Renewal motion proven (≥1 client renewed).
- [ ] Add‑on revenue proven (Tabletop, Threat Briefing, or AI Policy sold ≥1×).
- [ ] 10 active clients.

### **Horizon 3 — 60‑Agent Platform Vision**

Productize the engine. The internal SwarmCommand becomes the external platform.

- [ ] Agent inventory drafted (≤60 agents grouped by function).
- [ ] Architecture diagram v1 in `Architecture_Diagrams/`.
- [ ] Promotion path defined from `SwarmCommand_Engine/Experiments` → `Future_Platform_Agents` → platform.
- [ ] Multi‑tenant data model sketched (still Canadian‑hosted).
