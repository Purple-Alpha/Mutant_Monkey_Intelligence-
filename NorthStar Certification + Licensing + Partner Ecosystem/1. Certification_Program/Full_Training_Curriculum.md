# Full Training Curriculum
NSCO - NorthStar Certified Operator

## Week 1 - Foundations
- Human-layer security
- NorthStar service model
- Operator responsibilities
- Client communication basics

## Week 2 - Simulation Operations
- Template selection
- Delivery
- Click processing
- Repeat clicker handling
- Department mapping

## Week 3 - Training and Reporting
- Micro-training assignment
- Training completion tracking
- Leadership summary
- Evidence package
- Report generation

## Week 4 - Client Management
- Onboarding
- Client notes
- Renewal workflow
- Communication
- Escalation rules

## Practical Assignments
- Run a simulation.
- Process click data.
- Generate a report.
- Assemble evidence.

## Final Evaluation
The candidate must complete a final simulation run and pass the certification exam with a score of 80% or higher.
