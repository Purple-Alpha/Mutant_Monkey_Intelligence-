# Certification Handbook
NorthStar Certified Operator (NSCO) - Official Handbook

## 1. Program Overview
The NorthStar Certified Operator credential validates that an operator can independently deliver:
- Monthly phishing simulations
- Micro-training
- Human-risk reporting
- Evidence packages
- Renewal workflows

This certification is required for Licensed Providers and recommended for Partner Program members.

## 2. Certification Levels
- **NSCO - Operator:** core certification.
- **NSCO-P - Professional:** advanced reporting and evidence.
- **NSCO-E - Expert:** trainer and auditor level.

## 3. Learning Outcomes
Certified operators will be able to:
- Run simulations end-to-end.
- Process click data accurately.
- Assign and track micro-training.
- Generate monthly reports.
- Assemble evidence packages.
- Communicate with clients professionally.
- Maintain accurate client notes.
- Follow NorthStar quality standards.

## 4. Certification Requirements
To earn NSCO, candidates must:
1. Complete the training curriculum.
2. Pass the 50-question exam with a score of 80% or higher.
3. Complete 2 practical assignments.
4. Complete a final simulation run.

## 5. Renewal Requirements
- Annual renewal
- 10-question refresher quiz
- $99 renewal fee
- Review of updated templates

## 6. Code of Conduct
Certified operators must:
- Maintain accuracy.
- Follow evidence rules.
- Use approved templates.
- Maintain Canadian data residency.
- Communicate professionally.
- Protect client confidentiality.

## 7. Revocation Conditions
Certification may be revoked for:
- Data mishandling
- Misrepresentation
- Template modification
- Repeated quality failures
- Violation of licensing terms
