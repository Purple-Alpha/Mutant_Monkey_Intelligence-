# Partner Program Handbook
NorthStar Partner Program

## 1. Program Overview
The NorthStar Partner Program is for people and organizations that want to sell, refer, or help deliver NorthStar services.

## 2. Partner Tiers

### Silver Partner
- 20% recurring commission
- No minimum active-client requirement

### Gold Partner
- 30% recurring commission
- 5 active clients required

### Platinum Partner
- 40% recurring commission
- 15 active clients required
- Co-branding rights

## 3. Partner Benefits
- Sales deck
- Demo environment
- Partner portal
- Co-marketing
- Quarterly briefings

## 4. Partner Requirements
- Complete partner training.
- Follow brand rules.
- Do not misrepresent the service.
- Maintain active status.

## 5. Partner Sales Process
1. Discovery
2. Demo
3. Proposal
4. Close
5. Onboarding
6. Commission payout
