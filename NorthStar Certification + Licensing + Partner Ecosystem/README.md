# NorthStar Certification + Licensing + Partner Ecosystem

## Purpose
This folder contains the commercial ecosystem around NorthStar Security:
- Certification Program
- Licensing Program
- Partner Program
- Ecosystem monetization
- Partner portal structure

## Core Programs
- **NSCO - NorthStar Certified Operator:** trains and validates operators who deliver the NorthStar system.
- **NSLP - NorthStar Licensed Provider:** authorizes qualified providers to deliver, resell, or white-label NorthStar depending on tier.
- **NSPP - NorthStar Partner Program:** supports sales and referral partners who bring clients into the NorthStar ecosystem.

## How to Use This Folder
Use the program docs here to build training, sell licenses, onboard partners, and create future portal assets.

## Important Note
License agreement files are business drafts, not legal advice. Have qualified legal counsel review before signing or distributing externally.
