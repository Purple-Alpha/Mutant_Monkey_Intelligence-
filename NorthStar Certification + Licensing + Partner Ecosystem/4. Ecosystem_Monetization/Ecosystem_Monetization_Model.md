# Ecosystem Monetization Model

## 1. Certification Revenue
- $499-$1,499 per certification
- $99 annual renewal

## 2. Licensing Revenue
- Tier 1 Licensed Operator: $99/month
- Tier 2 Licensed Provider: $199/month
- Tier 3 Licensed Partner: $399/month

## 3. Partner Revenue
- NorthStar pays out 20-40% recurring commission.
- NorthStar keeps the remainder.
- Partners bring clients and expand reach.

## 4. Upsell Revenue
- Tabletop exercises
- Quarterly briefings
- AI usage policy
- Custom campaigns

## 5. Strategic Leverage
The ecosystem turns existing assets into recurring revenue:
- Operator Guide
- Workflows
- Templates
- Reporting system
- Evidence system
- Onboarding system
- Agent-based roadmap
