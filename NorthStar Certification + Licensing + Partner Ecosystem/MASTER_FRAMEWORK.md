# NorthStar Certification + Licensing + Partner Ecosystem
Master Framework

## Purpose
This framework turns NorthStar from a service business into an ecosystem with operator training, licensed delivery, partner sales, recurring revenue, and future platform leverage.

## Part 1 - NorthStar Certification Program
**NSCO - NorthStar Certified Operator**

The certification program trains and validates operators who can independently deliver:
- Monthly phishing simulations
- Micro-training for clickers
- Human-risk reporting
- Evidence package assembly
- Renewal workflows

## Part 2 - NorthStar Licensing Program
**NSLP - NorthStar Licensed Provider**

The licensing program allows qualified MSPs, consultants, trainers, and operators to deliver NorthStar under license.

## Part 3 - NorthStar Partner Program
**NSPP - NorthStar Sales + Delivery Partners**

The partner program allows sales partners and delivery partners to participate in NorthStar growth through referrals, commissions, co-selling, and qualified delivery.

## Part 4 - Monetization Model
Revenue sources:
- Certification fees
- Annual certification renewal
- Monthly license fees
- Partner-driven client revenue
- Upsells and add-ons

## Strategic Role
The ecosystem creates leverage around the existing NorthStar operating system:
- Operator Guide
- Business Operations Guide
- Delivery workflows
- Reporting templates
- Evidence system
- Client onboarding system
- SwarmCommand automation roadmap

This package contains first-draft program assets ready for refinement, legal review, branding, and future portal implementation.
