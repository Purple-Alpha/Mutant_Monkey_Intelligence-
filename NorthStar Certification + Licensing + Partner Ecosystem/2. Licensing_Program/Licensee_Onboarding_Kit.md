# Licensee Onboarding Kit
NorthStar Licensed Provider - Onboarding Package

## 1. Welcome Letter
Welcome to the NorthStar Licensing Program.

As a Licensed Provider, you are authorized to deliver NorthStar services using approved templates, workflows, reporting formats, and evidence standards.

## 2. What You Receive
- Branding kit
- Templates
- Updated monthly campaigns
- Reporting templates
- Evidence templates
- Operator guide
- Support channel access
- License agreement

## 3. Required Before Activation
Licensees must:
- Sign the License Agreement.
- Complete NSCO certification.
- Complete onboarding checklist.
- Set up client folders.
- Review reporting templates.

## 4. Onboarding Checklist
- Sign license agreement.
- Complete NSCO certification.
- Review operator guide.
- Review workflows.
- Review templates.
- Set up client folders.
- Configure simulation schedule.
- Configure training modules.
- Review evidence requirements.

## 5. Licensee Responsibilities
- Maintain certification.
- Follow reporting standards.
- Follow evidence rules.
- Maintain Canadian data residency.
- Use approved templates.
- Maintain quality standards.

## 6. Support Channels
- Email support
- Monthly office hours
- Quarterly updates
- Template update notifications
