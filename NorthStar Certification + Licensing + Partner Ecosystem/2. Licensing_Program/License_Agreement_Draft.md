## 1. Grant of License
Licensor grants Licensee a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to:
- Deliver NorthStar services.
- Use NorthStar templates.
- Use NorthStar reporting formats.
- Access monthly updated campaigns.
- Use NorthStar branding depending on tier.

This license does not transfer ownership of any intellectual property.

## 2. License Tiers

### Tier 1 - Licensed Operator
- Delivery rights only
- No resale rights
- $99/month

### Tier 2 - Licensed Provider
- Delivery and resale rights
- Access to updated templates
- $199/month

### Tier 3 - Licensed Partner
- Delivery, resale, and white-label rights
- Co-branding rights
- Quarterly briefings
- $399/month

## 3. Licensee Obligations
Licensee agrees to:
- Maintain NSCO certification.
- Follow reporting standards.
- Follow evidence rules.
- Maintain Canadian data residency.
- Use approved templates.
- Maintain quality standards.
- Protect client confidentiality.

## 4. Fees
Licensee agrees to pay the monthly fee for its selected tier.

Fees are billed monthly and are non-refundable unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## 5. Restrictions
Licensee may not:
- Redistribute templates.
- Sublicense the system.
- Modify core templates without approval.
- Misrepresent the service.
- Store data outside Canada.
- Reverse engineer any component.

## 6. Term and Renewal
- Term: 12 months
- Auto-renews unless cancelled according to the agreement terms
- Renewal fee may apply
- Licensor may update templates and workflows

## 7. Termination
Licensor may terminate immediately for:
- Non-payment
- Data mishandling
- Misuse of brand
- Repeated quality failures
- Violation of certification requirements

Upon termination, Licensee must:
- Cease all delivery.
- Delete all templates.
- Remove branding.
- Stop using NorthStar materials.

Licensor is not liable for:
