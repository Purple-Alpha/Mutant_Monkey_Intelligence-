# New Operator Training Guide
NorthStar Security - Operator Onboarding Manual

## 1. Welcome to NorthStar
NorthStar is a monthly human-layer security service for Canadian SMBs.

As an operator, your job is to deliver:
- Monthly phishing simulations
- Micro-training for clickers
- Monthly human-risk reports
- Leadership evidence packages

Everything you do supports these four outputs.

---

# 2. Your Core Responsibilities

## 2.1 Monthly Responsibilities
- Run the phishing simulation.
- Process click data.
- Assign micro-training.
- Track training completion.
- Generate the monthly report.
- Generate the leadership summary.
- Assemble the evidence package.
- Deliver everything to the client.

## 2.2 Weekly Responsibilities
- Check for overdue training.
- Review client notes.
- Update risk trends.
- Prepare for upcoming simulations.

## 2.3 Daily Responsibilities
- Check simulation inbox.
- Check training completions.
- Update client notes.
- Respond to client questions.

# 4. How to Run a Monthly Simulation

## 4.1 Before Simulation
- Verify client contact list.
- Confirm simulation schedule.
- Select template.
- Review last month's results.

## 4.2 During Simulation
- Deliver simulation.
- Monitor delivery.
- Watch for bounces.
- Watch for immediate clicks.

## 4.3 After Simulation
- Export click data.
- Identify repeat clickers.
- Update client notes.
- Assign micro-training.

# 11. Escalation Rules

## 11.1 Escalate to Founder
Escalate to Matt if:
- A client requests a custom campaign.
- A client requests a tabletop exercise.
- A client requests a policy review.
- A client requests a security audit.
- A client is at risk of churn.
- A client wants to upgrade.

## 11.2 Escalate Internally
Escalate internally if:
- Evidence is incomplete.
- Training is overdue.
- A simulation fails.
- A report is delayed.

# 12. Operator Success Metrics

You are doing your job well if:
- All simulations run on time.
- All training is assigned within 24 hours.
- All reports are delivered by month-end.
- All evidence packages are complete.
- Client notes are always up to date.
- No client asks, "Where is my report?"

# 13. Operator FAQ

## What if a client does not complete training?
Send reminders weekly. Escalate after 3 weeks.

## What if a simulation bounces?
Update the contact list and notify the client.

## What if a client wants a custom report?
Escalate to Matt.

## What if a client wants to pause service?
Log it and notify Matt immediately.
