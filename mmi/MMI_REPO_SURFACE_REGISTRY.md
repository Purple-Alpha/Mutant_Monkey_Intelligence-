2. **No inference** that `BUILD_AUTHORIZATION_IMPLIED: YES` means operator build authorization (see review material invariant I13).
3. **No inference** that `RECOMMENDED_DIRECTION` or top delegation score authorizes work (recommendation is not authorization).
4. **No inference** from schema/template files (F2–F4) that live registry, appendix, or automation exists.
5. **No cross-repo inference** — Architectapp or external lanes are not swarm build surfaces unless separate operator authorization.
