## 1. Objective

Define a **Phase B/C platform architecture** where:

1. **Pneumatic Lung** — each tenant cell inflates and deflates elastic defensive capacity under governed load and threat pressure.
2. **Immune Federation Mesh** — opted-in tenant cells share **sanitized campaign warning** without sharing inboxes, evidence, or investigation truth.
3. **Network economics** — as more MSP clients join a federation pool, marginal cost to recognize a **repeat campaign** drops from deep synthesis toward cheap deterministic verification (not “free defense”).

This is **not** cyber-insurance GTM, **not** a Barracuda replacement, and **not** literal cross-tenant agent migration into another company’s mail.

## 3. Vision — synchronized immune response (not shared inbox)

When **Company A** faces a coordinated campaign:

1. Local shadow sensors and HQ classify pressure; Lung may inflate **inside A’s namespace**.
2. A **Pathogen Signature Pulse** (sanitized, bounded, TTL’d) may emit to an **opted-in MSP federation mesh** — if local threshold and ReconciliationAgent `#84` egress rules pass.
3. **Companies B and C** do **not** read A’s mail. They receive only non-reversible indicators and ask locally: *“Do I see structurally similar threat shape?”*
4. B and C may **inflate locally**, run cheap deterministic matching, or pre-harden — per local Mode Controller mesh mode and mesh trust tier.
5. When the campaign ends, **all cells exhale**; agents return to baseline; pulse expires.

**Operator phrase:**

```text
Shared threat shape.
Never shared tenant truth.
```

## 4. Piggyback reinterpretation (governed)

Original operator intuition: B and C “piggyback” onto A to multiply the swarm.

**Governed translation:**

- Agents **do not leave their host tenant** to inspect another tenant’s evidence.
- The **platform** may allocate pooled cloud compute to the originating cell under network billing rules.
- **Pattern benefit** propagates mesh-wide; **data** does not.

This preserves Guardrail 11 while delivering the economic and defensive multiplier Matt intended.

---

## 5. MSP / Todd economics (concept only — no prices locked)

**Pitch shape (not authorization):**

- MSPs join a **federation pool** (e.g. regional mesh under Todd).
- More opted-in tenants → lower **platform compute unit** cost for repeat-campaign recognition.
- First tenant on a novel campaign pays synthesis + evidence review cost.
- Subsequent tenants benefit from bounded matching and reduced redundant deep analysis.

**Honest economic rule (see hardening addendum §11):**

Marginal cost drops toward **deterministic verification**, not zero.

Pricing tables, rev-share, and contracts require separate operator butterfly decisions.
