## 6. Why success definition is not ready yet

Per Matt's candidate view:

- The autonomous intelligence / brain layer is not finished as a complete, signed, governed system.
- Immune-system / health-layer concepts are not yet doctrine or build scope.
- Swarm fission, swarm lung, and dual-LLM purple-team concepts remain future candidates.
- Personality, adaptive tolerance, leniency, and "being" are emergence questions that presuppose lower layers.

Therefore **final MMI success cannot be defined now** without laundering future ambition into present authority.
