## 4. Core concept (candidate framing only)

**Candidate idea (not authorized):** A future MMI might behave less like a static control panel and more like an **adaptive cyber-intelligence organism** — sensing adversary behavior, maintaining internal health boundaries, adapting within signed constraints, and converting intelligence into defensive decision logic without collapsing into hash worship or unconstrained autonomy.

**Artificial-life / organism language** in this intake is **metaphor and exploration only**. It may help Matt reason about:

- durable behavior/TTP intelligence vs perishable indicators (see `mmi/research/MMI_CYBER_SECURITY_INTELLIGENCE_INPUT_APT29_ENVYSCOUT.md`)
- phased evolution of brain → immune system → swarm lung/fission → emergence questions (see `mmi/research/MMI_SUCCESS_EVOLUTION_ROADMAP_INTAKE.md`)
- compartmentalized organs, fail-closed recovery, and evidence discipline (related advisory material exists separately; not adopted here)

**Intake rule:** This concept explores **identity and architecture imagination**. It does not select a build path.

The organism / artificial-life framing is **compelling**. Compelling concepts are a known drift vector: they can feel architecturally true before repo evidence, signed contracts, or falsifiable tests exist.

Skeptical review was requested **because persuasion risk is high**, not because the concept is dismissed. The purpose is to prevent:
