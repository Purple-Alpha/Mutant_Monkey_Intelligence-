## 5. Why cybersecurity is too narrow as a whole-project label

Cybersecurity is a **current serious battlefield** with real detectors, evidence packages, swarm layers, and customer-relevant work.

But labeling the entire project "cybersecurity" would:

- collapse exploration into one present lane
- hide swarm/body architecture, adaptive intelligence, and fission evolution
- encourage premature product identity and scoreboard narrowing
- risk laundering today's build queue into eternal project purpose

Cybersecurity remains important. It is **not** the whole lineage.
