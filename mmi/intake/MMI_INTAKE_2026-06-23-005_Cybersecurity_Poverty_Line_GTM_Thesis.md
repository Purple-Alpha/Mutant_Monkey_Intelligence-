## 1. Operator thesis (Matt — captured verbatim in spirit)

Small business is not a downgrade tier. A 25-person Canadian company needs Barracuda-**quality** inbox fraud defense but cannot pay corporate entry fees or SOC salaries. Enterprise vendors design for mid-market (100–1000 users) with minimum-seat walls and TCO that excludes StatCan **small** businesses (5–99 employees) and **micro** businesses (1–4).

**Mutant Monkey must:**

- **Smash** per-seat prices — not nibble ($3.99 vs $5); cross the poverty line so MSPs notice
- Deliver **quality in the evidence chain** — receipts on every flag; BEC/vendor wire fraud beside Defender
- Use **federation pool economics** so repeat-campaign recognition gets cheaper at MSP scale
- Stay **beside Defender** — fill BEC/social-engineering gap; do not claim endpoint replacement
- Win through **MSP channel** (Canadian MSPs like Todd path) — direct enterprise sales is not the lane

**Not claiming:** beating Barracuda feature parity; corporate league pricing; NorthStar identity

## 2. Cybersecurity Poverty Line (industry frame)

Widely recognized structural gap (Sophos *Cybersecurity Poverty Line* + sector literature):

| Wall | Mechanism | SMB impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum Seat Wall** | 50–500 seat quote floors; Barracuda $3.40–6.50/user mid-market packaging | 15-employee shop buys via MSP markup or goes without |
| **TCO Wall** | License + alert volume + SOC analyst ($80k–110k CAD) | True cost 200–400% above sticker; micro/small cannot staff SOC |

**Market split:**

| Tier | Canada (employees) | Typical security spend | Problem |
|---|---|---|---|
| Micro / small | 1–99 (StatCan) | $1k–10k/yr retail AV or MSP bundle | Too basic OR overpriced repackaged enterprise |
| Mid-market gap | 25–250 | $10k–100k forced MSP/MDR | Outgrown AV; cannot afford direct enterprise |
| Enterprise | 500+ | $500k+ | Overpaying; not Mutant Monkey primary GTM |

## 3. Why enterprise stack falls short (inbox wedge)

Mutant Monkey addresses gaps **without** replacing M365/Defender:

1. **Keys to the castle** — BEC logs in or impersonates; no malicious attachment for filter to catch
2. **Alert fatigue** — explain-without-blocking; digest posture; not 10k SOC alerts
3. **Misconfiguration / weak defaults** — add investigator layer Defender misses on thread/auth/look-alike
4. **No business context** — Dial 1 documented override when evidence says legit ($50k deal path)
5. **Defender BEC blind spot** — vendor wire fraud, CEO gift-card, look-alike domain mid-thread
