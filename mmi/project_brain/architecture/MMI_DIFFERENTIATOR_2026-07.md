## Seven structural differences

### 1. Falsifiable milestones (you can fail on purpose)

Most AGI roadmaps are unfalsifiable vibes. MMI defines pass lines a script can measure:

| Phase | Pass line (summary) |
|-------|---------------------|
| Phase 1 | 3 consecutive Tier-4 weapon lab runs |
| Phase 2 | ≥9/10 novel formats; zero integrity-gate bypasses |
| Phase 3 | 30 days, 0% leakage, 100% proof bundles at sign-off |

**Status:** Phase 1 **PASS** on record. Phases 2–3 **PLANNED**.

### 2. Proof before signature (not human-in-the-loop theater)

Generic: model proposes → human clicks approve.

MMI: human signs an **evidence bundle** — patch hash, proof-of-fix, proof-of-no-regression, rollback token. The console gate **rejects** signature on failed or missing proofs. Alignment binds machine **and** operator haste.

**Status:** Console Ed25519 gate **PLANNED**; ops closeout gates **BUILT** (Track B).

### 3. The metadata iceberg (judge what attackers cannot see)

Normal envelopes travel **on the packet** — forgeable if the format is known.

MMI depth layers (L4–L9) are **gate-derived** from history the sender never receives: fingerprint, stream correlation, lane graph, rhythm, canaries, chain depth. A leaked signing key does not equal a breach.

**Status:** Tip + L8 + L6 + L4 + L5 **BUILT**; Phases 2–3 **PLANNED**.

### 4. Suspicion routes; proof kills

| Layer class | On anomaly |
|-------------|------------|
| L4/L5/L7 behavioral | Route to **Mirror Dimension** — inspect, do not hard-drop |
| L8 canary | **Hard-drop** — proven hostile signal |

Legitimate agents do new things. Killing on first deviation breaks the swarm. Most systems over-block or under-react; MMI splits *suspicious* from *proven hostile*.

### 5. Equal battlefield scoring

Purple evasion and weapon scoring share one matrix. No demo-only pass rates. Tier 4 means Tier 4 on `MMI_WEAPON_BATTLEFIELD_SCORING_MATRIX_v1`, not because an agent said PASS.

**Status:** Scorer + purple suite **BUILT**; Phase 1 stability harness **PASS**.

### 6. Bounded autonomy (explicit non-goals)

MMI does **not** pursue unbounded self-authored runtime code. Phase 2 is **bounded, typed schemas** validated by `action_integrity_gate.py` before execution. That is the opposite of most "agent swarm" marketing.

**Status:** Action integrity gate v1 **BUILT**; `central_brain.py` **PLANNED**.

### 7. Honest two-track execution

| Track | What | Status |
|-------|------|--------|
| **A — Weapon** | Controlled chaos, lung, mirror, polyglot, Assume-Click | Doctrine **FILED**; partial chaos **BUILT** |
| **B — Ops** | Closeout gates, backup, war room, OPSEC | **BUILT** P1–P8 |

MMI does not claim the weapon is done because ops is green. **PARKED ≠ rejected** — concepts stay on the roadmap until chaos-lab falsification or Matt retires them.
